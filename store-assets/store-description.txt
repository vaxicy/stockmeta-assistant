=== 中文说明 ===

StockMeta Assistant 是 Adobe Stock 供稿人的上传助手：在上传页一键生成素材标题和关键词并填入表单，支持批量处理、红点素材清理与中英双语。可配置自己的视觉模型；设置和 API Key 仅存本机，素材不会被自动提交。

=== English Description ===

StockMeta Assistant is an upload helper for Adobe Stock contributors. Generate titles and keywords and fill them into the upload form in one click, batch-process assets, clean up red-flagged items, and switch between Chinese and English. Configure your own vision model; settings and API keys stay on your device, and assets are never submitted automatically.
