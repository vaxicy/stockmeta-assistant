=== 中文说明 ===

StockMeta Assistant 是 Adobe Stock 供稿人的 AI 文案助手：在素材上传页用你配置的视觉模型读取当前素材，生成描述性标题与关键词，一键填入表单；也可以一次点击，为整批待处理素材依次生成并填入。界面中英双语，支持多家 AI 服务商与自定义 OpenAI 兼容端点，API Key 仅保存在本地，不代你提交。

=== English Description ===

StockMeta Assistant is an AI copywriting helper for Adobe Stock contributors: on the upload page it reads the asset you are working on, has the vision model you configure write a descriptive title and keywords, and fills them into the form in one click — or handles a whole batch of pending assets from a single click. It works with several AI providers or your own OpenAI-compatible endpoint, the panel is bilingual (Chinese / English), your API key stays on your device, and it never submits for you.
