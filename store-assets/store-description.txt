=== 中文详细说明 ===

StockMeta Assistant 是一款专为 Adobe Stock 供稿人打造的 AI 辅助插件。它读取你正在上传的素材（图片或插画），借助视觉模型自动生成贴合平台的标题与关键词，并一键填入 Adobe Stock 表单，帮你把几分钟的重复录入压缩到一次点击。

核心功能
- AI 视觉识别：直接分析当前素材，自动生成描述性标题与最多 50 个关键词，覆盖主体、场景、情绪与风格。
- 多 AI 提供商：内置 SiliconFlow、OpenAI 以及自定义（OpenAI 兼容）端点，自由切换并填写自己的 API Key。
- 字段级重新生成：标题或关键词旁边各带一个刷新按钮，可单独重新生成其中一项，不必重做全部。
- 一键填入：支持「应用标题」「应用关键词」「全部应用」三种粒度，也支持单独复制文本到剪贴板。
- 自动勾选 AI 声明：应用元数据时可自动勾选 Adobe Stock 表单上的「使用生成式 AI 工具创建」与「人物与财产均为虚构」两项声明（可选）。
- 应用后自动保存：应用标题或关键词后自动点击页面上的「保存工作」按钮（可选）。
- 中英双语界面：界面随浏览器语言或手动选择在中文 / 英文之间切换。
- 可拖拽、可折叠侧边面板：生成结果常驻在 Adobe Stock 页面右侧，不遮挡你的编辑区。
- 弹出页快捷入口：一键跳转到 Adobe Stock 上传页面，并展示连接状态。
- 隐私安全：API Key 仅保存在浏览器本地 chrome.storage.local，绝不上传或硬编码到扩展中。

StockMeta Assistant 只负责把生成好的文案填入表单，不会自动提交或发布你的作品，最终审核与发布仍由你掌控。

=== English Detailed Description ===

StockMeta Assistant is an AI helper built for Adobe Stock contributors. It reads the asset you are uploading (a photo or illustration), uses a vision model to generate a fitting title and keywords, and fills them into the Adobe Stock form in one click, turning minutes of repetitive typing into a single action.

Key features
- AI vision recognition: analyzes your current asset and automatically generates a descriptive title plus up to 50 keywords covering subject, scene, mood, and style.
- Multiple AI providers: built-in SiliconFlow, OpenAI, and a custom (OpenAI-compatible) endpoint, so you can switch freely and bring your own API Key.
- Per-field regeneration: a refresh button sits next to the title and the keywords, letting you regenerate just one of them without redoing everything.
- One-click fill: choose "Apply Title", "Apply Keywords", or "Apply All", and copy either field to the clipboard on its own.
- Auto-check AI declaration: when applying metadata, optionally tick the "Created using generative AI tools" and "People and Property are fictional" boxes on the Adobe Stock form.
- Auto-save after Apply: optionally click the "Save work" button on Adobe Stock right after applying your title or keywords.
- Bilingual interface: the UI follows your browser language or switches manually between Chinese and English.
- Draggable, collapsible side panel: results stay pinned to the right side of the Adobe Stock page without blocking your editing area.
- Popup shortcut: jump straight to the Adobe Stock uploads page and see your connection status at a glance.
- Privacy first: your API Key is stored only in the browser's local chrome.storage.local, never uploaded or hardcoded into the extension.

StockMeta Assistant only fills the generated text into the form. It never auto-submits or publishes your work, so you stay in full control of review and release.
