=== 中文详细说明 ===

StockMeta Assistant 是 Adobe Stock 供稿人的 AI 文案助手：读取你正在上传的素材，用视觉模型生成标题与关键词，一键填入表单。

- AI 视觉生成：分析当前素材，产出描述性标题与最多 50 个关键词，覆盖主体、场景与风格
- 四种 AI 提供商：SiliconFlow、OpenAI、Gemini、自定义 OpenAI 兼容端点，填入自己的 API Key 即用
- 素材类型与类别：自动识别 Photos / Illustrations 并填入，默认值在任意「应用」按钮下都生效，Adobe 已识别时保留（可选）
- 精细控制：标题、关键词可单独重新生成；三种粒度填入，也可一键复制
- 可选自动化：自动勾选「使用生成式 AI 工具创建」声明、应用后自动保存
- 中英双语界面，可拖拽、可折叠面板，不遮挡编辑区；API Key 仅存本地，绝不上传

=== English Detailed Description ===

StockMeta Assistant is an AI copywriting helper for Adobe Stock contributors: it reads the asset you are uploading, generates a title and keywords with a vision model, and fills the form in one click.

- AI vision: analyzes the current asset and produces a descriptive title plus up to 50 keywords covering subject, scene, and style
- Four AI providers: SiliconFlow, OpenAI, Gemini, or any custom OpenAI-compatible endpoint — just bring your own API Key
- Asset type & category: auto-detects Photos / Illustrations and fills them in; your defaults apply from any Apply button and are kept when Adobe already detected a value (optional)
- Fine control: regenerate the title or keywords separately; apply at three granularities or copy with one click
- Optional automation: auto-tick the "Created using generative AI" declaration and auto-save after applying
- Bilingual UI with a draggable, collapsible panel that never blocks your editing area; your API Key stays local and is never uploaded
